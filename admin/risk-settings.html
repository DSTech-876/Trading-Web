Risk Settings

Configure the platform-wide Daily Loss Limit used to halt auto-trading (web app and MT5 bridge EA).

Current Daily Loss Limit: —
%
Trading halts for the rest of the day once the account's drawdown from its day-start value reaches this percentage. The MT5 bridge computes this from live account equity (including unrealized P/L); the web engine computes it from account balance. Allowed range: 0.1%–100%.
