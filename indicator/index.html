    🌊 Strategy 1: Liquidity Sweep (15m → 1m)

    Identifies range from a single candle's high/low. If the next candle breaks the range but closes back inside → liquidity sweep signal. Entry at candle close, SL just outside the range, TP at 2:1 R:R. Works best on 15m/1m charts for scalp entries.

    🎯 Strategy 2: Stop Loss Hunt

    Finds key support/resistance levels tested multiple times. When price breaks beyond the level but closes back inside → stop hunt signal. Entry at candle close, SL beyond the hunt candle, TP at 2:1 R:R. Includes re-entry logic if your stop is hunted again (stop hunt of stop hunters).

    📌 Strategy 3: Failed Pin Bar (Fear/Greed)

    Detects market fear (consecutive bearish candles) or greed (consecutive bullish candles). Spots a pin bar against the dominant emotion, then enters when the pin bar fails (breaks in direction of momentum). SL beyond the pin bar, TP at 1.5:1 R:R for quick scalp pips.

    📐 Strategy 4: Fib Golden Zone Scalp

    Pure price action + Fibonacci on the 1-minute chart. Identifies a micro-trend (higher lows / lower highs), waits for a break of structure, then enters when price retraces into the 0.5–0.618 Fibonacci retracement zone (the "Golden Zone"). Exits at the previous swing high (uptrend) or swing low (downtrend). SL beyond the 0.786 Fib level. Fast, clean 10-minute scalp strategy.

    ⚡ Strategy 5: Power of 3 (ICT)

    ICT Power of 3 delivery: Accumulation → Manipulation → Expansion. Uses EMA 8/21 daily bias to define direction. Marks the 1-hour candle open, waits for a liquidity sweep (manipulation) beyond the open, then waits for a market structure shift (MSS) with displacement that leaves a Fair Value Gap (FVG). Enters when price returns into the FVG. SL below the sweep low (bullish) or above the sweep high (bearish). TP at the 1H high/low + external liquidity. Partial at 1–2R, let the rest run.

    Controls how recent the last FVG touch must be before a PO3 alert is fired. 0-candle mode only alerts on the current touch candle.

    🔲 Strategy 8: Grid Scalper MA

    Dynamic MA-based signal strategy with three modes: Price vs MA fires when price closes above/below a Simple Moving Average crossover, BOS fires when price breaks above a confirmed swing high (BUY) or below a confirmed swing low (SELL), and Triple MA uses SMA 50 as trend filter, SMA 20 as directional confirmation, and SMA 11 as entry timing via pullback-rejection. SL placed at the nearest swing point + ATR buffer. TP at 2:1 R:R.

    Select signal generation mode. "Price vs MA" uses a Simple Moving Average crossover. "BOS" detects confirmed swing high/low breakouts. "Triple MA" requires price below SMA 50 & SMA 20 for SELL (or above for BUY), then enters on a pullback rejection at SMA 11.

    Period for the Simple Moving Average (default: 21). Shorter = more signals, longer = stronger trends only.

    🎯 Strategy 9: Fair Value Gap (FVG)
    
              
    ICT-style supply & demand strategy using Fair Value Gaps as confluence. Detects a "big push" (3+ consecutive strong candles) that leaves FVG imbalances, then marks the origin candle (the demand/supply zone before the push). Waits for price to retrace back into that zone at a Fibonacci discount (<50% retracement), confirms with a bullish/bearish engulfing pattern, and enters in the direction of the original push. SL below the demand zone (or above supply). TP at the recent swing high/low. Requires EMA trend alignment. Works on any timeframe.
    

              
    
              
    🕓 Strategy 15: 4H Power of 3 (HDF PO3) Liquidity Play
    
              
    Uses the HDF Power 3 indicator concept on 4H candle structure. Identifies the 4H candle open, detects accumulation (sideways), manipulation (liquidity sweep below/above accumulation), and distribution via CISD (Change in State of Delivery) — a displacement candle that shifts structure back in the HTF bias direction. Entry after CISD confirmation. SL below the manipulation low (bullish) / above manipulation high (bearish). TP at minimum 2:1 R:R or nearest liquidity (4H candle high/low). Only trades in the direction of higher-timeframe EMA bias.
    

              
    
              
    🧱 Strategy 16: 1H Accumulation Break + Breaker Block Scalping
    
              
    Identifies 1H accumulation candles (large body, small wicks) and marks their high/low as liquidity targets. On the execution timeframe, waits for a liquidity sweep (break above high or below low), then identifies a breaker block structure: Bullish breaker = Low → High → Lower Low → Higher High (after low swept); Bearish breaker = High → Low → Higher High → Lower Low (after high swept). Enters on retest of the breaker block zone. SL just beyond breaker extreme. TP at 2:1 R:R. Designed for fast scalping execution.
